Privacy Policy for Stopwatch Time Counter

Effective date: June 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Stopwatch Time Counter handles user data.

App name: Stopwatch Time Counter
Package name: com.juicedaninja.timer
Developer: Juice
Privacy contact: Use the developer contact information listed for this app on Google Play to submit privacy questions or requests.

Summary

Stopwatch Time Counter is a stopwatch and timer app for start/stop timing, lap timing, race finish timing, saved timer records, dark/light mode, and user-initiated copying of saved records to the device clipboard.

Stopwatch Time Counter does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal or sensitive user data. The app stores timer records and app preferences locally on your device only.

User Data Accessed, Collected, Used, or Shared

Stopwatch Time Counter does not collect user data from your device and does not transmit user data off your device.

The app does not require an account and does not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, location data, contacts, photos, videos, audio, camera data, microphone data, financial information, health information, authentication information, device identifiers, advertising identifiers, diagnostics, crash logs, installed app lists, web browsing data, or analytics data.

The app does not share user data with third parties and does not sell personal or sensitive user data.

Local App Data

Stopwatch Time Counter stores the following data locally on your device:

  • Timer records you create, including stopwatch records, lap records, race finish records, dates, and timer text.
  • App preferences, such as dark/light mode.

This local data is used only to display your timer history and app settings inside Stopwatch Time Counter. It is not uploaded to a server, not sent to the developer, and not shared with third parties.

Clipboard

Stopwatch Time Counter can copy saved record text to your device clipboard when you tap a copy button. Clipboard use is user-initiated. After you copy text, clipboard contents are controlled by your device operating system and by any apps where you choose to paste the copied text.

Permissions and Third-Party Code

Stopwatch Time Counter does not request sensitive Android permissions. The current app does not request internet, location, camera, microphone, contacts, calendar, files, photos, videos, audio, phone, SMS, or nearby-device permissions.

The app does not include advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, crash reporting SDKs, cloud sync, account login, or third-party tracking tools.

Security

Because Stopwatch Time Counter does not transmit user data, there is no user data encrypted in transit. Local app data remains on your device and is protected by Android app storage protections.

Data Retention and Deletion

Saved timer records remain on your device until you delete them.

You can delete individual saved records inside the app, delete all saved records inside the app, clear the app's storage in Android settings, or uninstall the app to remove local app data.

Accounts

Stopwatch Time Counter does not allow users to create accounts. Account deletion is not applicable because the app has no account system.

Children's Privacy

Stopwatch Time Counter does not knowingly collect personal information from children or from any other users.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the app's features or data practices change. The effective date above will be updated when changes are made.
