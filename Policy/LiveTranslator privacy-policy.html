Privacy Policy

Live Translator by JuiceDaNinja

Last updated: September 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Live Translator handles user and device data. Live Translator is an Android app that translates spoken language into text in another language. The app is designed for live, multi-language translation.

Developer and Contact

Developer: JuiceDaNinja

Privacy contact: privacy@JuiceDaNinja.com

Information the App Accesses

Live Translator accesses the device microphone only when you grant microphone permission and choose to start listening in the app. Microphone access is used to recognize spoken language so the app can show recognized text and translated text in the language pair you choose.

The app also uses internet access to prepare or download on-device language and translation models when needed. After the required models are prepared, supported speech recognition and translation features are designed to run on the device.

Information We Collect

Live Translator does not require an account and does not collect, sell, or store personal information on developer-controlled servers.

  • Audio is processed on the device for speech recognition.
  • Recognized speech text and translated text are shown in the app UI.
  • The app does not upload microphone audio, recognized text, or translations to developer-controlled servers.
  • The app does not include advertising or analytics features.

How Information Is Used

Microphone audio is used only to provide the live speech recognition feature. Recognized speech text is used only to produce translated text for the selected language pair. Internet access is used to prepare on-device language and translation models and support app functionality.

Sharing

We do not sell personal or sensitive user data. We do not share microphone audio, recognized speech, or translations with advertisers, analytics providers, or developer-controlled servers.

The app may use third-party libraries and services, including on-device speech recognition libraries and Google ML Kit / Google Play services for on-device translation model management. Google services may process technical information needed to download, update, and manage language or translation models according to Google's applicable privacy practices. The app is not designed to send your audio or translated text to Google for translation.

Data Retention and Deletion

Live Translator does not create an account and does not store user data on developer-controlled servers. Any app data stored locally on your device, such as prepared speech, language, or translation model files, can be removed by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app.

Security

Live Translator is designed to process speech recognition and translation on the device after required models are available. When the app or its SDKs need network access for model preparation or updates, data is transmitted using platform and SDK security mechanisms such as HTTPS where applicable.

Children's Privacy

Live Translator is not directed to children under 13. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app's data practices change. The updated policy will show a new "Last updated" date.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or Live Translator's privacy practices, contact: privacy@JuiceDaNinja.com.
